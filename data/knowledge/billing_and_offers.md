# Invoices and price changes

## Invoice failures
A failed invoice is a lapse risk even when cabinets are in use. Confirm the bill-to contact, the purchase order, and whether the clinic rejected a line. Clear that before the term date.

## Price changes
A price change is allowed only after service tickets and stocking gaps are assigned an owner. Tie the change to a commitment: a stocking completion date, a minimum monthly order, or a named buyer. Do not repeat a blanket discount if a previous discount did not bring orders back.

## What not to offer
Do not credit a clinic for unused cabinets without a stocking plan. Do not promise same-day delivery that the route cannot keep.
