# Clinic supply term playbook

## Service review
When a clinic has several open service tickets, fix the shipments before talking about price. Name an owner, record the short or damaged items, and give the clinic the next delivery date. A health system with a down cabinet in a procedure room gets a same-week call.

## Order recovery
A drop in standing orders means the clinic is buying around the agreement or the par level is wrong. Compare the last 30 days with this clinic's own baseline, pick the two cabinets that fell furthest, and reset those pars. Do not offer a discount as the first step.

## Term readiness
Inside 60 days of term, review fill rate, backorders, cabinet use, invoice issues, and who the buyer is. Show that packet before any price conversation. A price change needs approval and a clinic commitment, such as a stocking date or a minimum order.

## Director review
Bring the medical director in when the contract value is high, the term is close, or the same service failure has happened twice. State the owner and the next date. Do not promise a credit that has not been approved.
