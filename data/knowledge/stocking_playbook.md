# Cabinet stocking playbook

## First stocking
A new clinic is not live until each contracted cabinet has a par level, a named buyer, and one completed replenishment. Walk the rooms. Do not mark stocking complete from the contract signature alone.

## Idle cabinets
If the active cabinet ratio is low, find the rooms that never placed an order. Common causes are a missing barcode, a buyer who left, or a formulary item the clinic does not use. Remove or swap those items instead of adding a discount.

## Buyer coverage
Every cabinet needs a buyer and a backup. A single buyer across a health system is a lapse risk when that person is out. Write the names into the clinic record before the term review.
